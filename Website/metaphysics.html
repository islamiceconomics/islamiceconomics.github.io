Research programme

The Metaphysics of Islamic Economics

An inquiry into the metaphysical foundations of Islamic economic thought

The central question

The Economy of the Seen and the Unseen

Economic analysis gives careful attention to households, firms, markets, institutions, and measurable outcomes. Islamic economic thought also considers realities, purposes, and moral commitments that are not fully captured by measurement, but that can still shape economic life.

This is why the unseen matters to economic inquiry. The project asks how it relates to the economy we observe and how that relationship should be described.

Research questions

Six Concepts Under Study

Each raises a different question about the unseen, human agency, and observable economic life. The set is a starting point rather than a complete inventory.

01

Determination

Qadar

How can economic events be determined without making human striving meaningless?

02

Agency

Niyyah

How can intention enter the reality of an act rather than merely accompany it?

03

Provision

Rizq

How should provision be understood when income captures only one of its measurable forms?

04

Quality

Barakah

How can the same measured quantity bear different degrees of goodness and sufficiency?

05

Moral form

Halal and Haram

How does permissibility shape the form, conduct, and consequences of economic exchange?

06

Structure

Riba

What metaphysical account of economic claims and exchange might help explain the prohibition of riba?

The long-form work

The Treatise

The manuscript develops this framework as a sixteen-chapter study. It establishes an Islamic account of the seen and the unseen, then examines the metaphysical assumptions carried by central economic concepts.

Work in Progress
  1. I

    The Question

    Distinguishing economic models from metaphysical inquiry and establishing the two-layer ontology.

  2. II

    The Apparatus

    Developing the conceptual vocabulary needed to articulate the relation without reducing it to a model.

  3. III

    Worked Treatments

    Asking what intention, determination, provision, blessing, lawful exchange, and riba are.

  4. IV

    Synthesis

    Drawing the concepts together and identifying questions for later formal economic theory.
