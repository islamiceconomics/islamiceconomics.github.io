\vspace*{4in}
\noindent\textit{This is a working draft of Volume 0, Part I, of the
Hidden Architecture project. The complete volume will treat eight
canonical metaphysical claims of the Islamic economic tradition with the
mathematical and philosophical rigor those claims have not yet
received in the literature. Subsequent volumes will build the formal
microeconomic, macroeconomic, financial, and policy theory on these
foundations.}
\vspace{0.5in}

\noindent\textit{Comments, criticisms, and corrections are sought.
The work is intended as a contribution to a field that does not yet
exist; its development requires the engagement of others.}
