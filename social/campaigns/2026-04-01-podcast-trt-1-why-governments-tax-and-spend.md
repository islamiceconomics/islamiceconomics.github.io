## LinkedIn

Why do governments exist economically? Markets excel at producing private goods but systematically underprovide public goods like defense, infrastructure, and clean water. The free rider problem means voluntary contributions don't work at scale. The early Islamic state offers a fascinating case study. Under Abu Bakr and Umar, the caliphate built a fiscal system from scratch: zakat collection, kharaj on agricultural land, fay' from conquered territories, all channeled through the bayt al-mal. The foundational principle was amana: public revenue is a trust held on behalf of the people, not the property of rulers. A 1,400-year-old solution to market failure.
