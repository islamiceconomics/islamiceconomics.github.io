Why does a worker in Germany produce ten times more per hour than a worker in Bangladesh? It's not effort or intelligence—it's capital, technology, and institutions. This productivity puzzle becomes more intriguing when you consider history. For five centuries, the Islamic world was the global productivity leader. Today, Muslim-majority countries are disproportionately in the lower-middle and low-income categories.
